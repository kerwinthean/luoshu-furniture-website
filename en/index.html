Why Work With Us

A direct factory relationship, not a trading layer.

Direct from the factory

You deal with the people who make the beds. No trading company margin, and no guessing about what is actually possible on the line.

Any size, any market

Our standard is 180x200cm. We regularly build to US Queen and King, UK sizes, and EU metric sizes. Send us your dimensions.

Fabric and leather

Woven fabric, PU and genuine leather. Send a swatch or a reference photo and we will match it.

Live factory tour

Ask for a video call and we will walk you through the workshop, the timber, the foam and the packing area.

Container-efficient packing

Beds are knocked down and flat packed at roughly 0.8-1.2 CBM per set, so one 40HQ carries 54-81 units depending on the model.

The Foshan supply chain

Our factory sits in Foshan, the largest furniture cluster in the world. Fabric, foam, hardware and timber frames are all within half an hour of the workshop. That is why we can run small batches, sample fast and hold short lead times.

OEM and private label

Neutral packing as standard. Your brand on the labels and cartons on request.

How to Order

A first order usually runs on this sequence.

Send your enquiry

Tell us the models you like, your market, and the sizes you need. A rough quantity helps us price properly.

We quote

You receive an FOB quotation with sizes, upholstery options, packing, container quantity and lead time.

Sample if needed

We can produce a sample before bulk. Sample cost and freight are payable, and the sample cost is refunded against your order.

Confirm the PI

We issue a proforma invoice. Signing it and paying the deposit puts the order into production.

Production

Typically 30 days. We send progress photos so you know where your order stands.

Inspection and loading

We photograph and film the goods and the container loading before it sails.

Shipping

Your forwarder or ours. You receive the bill of lading and full document set.

About Luoshu

Foshan Luoshu Furniture Co., Ltd is a Chinese manufacturer of upholstered beds. We have spent years supplying demanding domestic brands, which means our finishing standard is set by customers who inspect every unit.

We are now opening the same production capacity to export buyers. Our focus markets are the Middle East and Europe, where our style of generously padded, tall-headboard beds suits local taste.

We are a factory, not an agent. If you want to see the line before you commit, ask us for a video tour.
